iFiFundza AI · Project presentation
Project concept · Proposed integrations

Learning without language barriers

Understand Maths & Science in the language you think in.

iFundza AI is a learning-support concept that helps South African high school learners understand questions in their home language.

MathematicsPhysical SciencesGrades 8–12AI-assisted learning

01 · The problem

Language can get in the way of learning.

A learner may understand the subject but still struggle to interpret a question written in a language they are less comfortable using.

01

Questions are text-heavy

Exam and textbook instructions can be difficult to unpack quickly.

02

Meaning matters

Misunderstanding a word can hide what a Maths or Science question is asking.

03

Notation must stay exact

Translation must not alter equations, units, marks, or scientific symbols.

02 · The solution

From a question photo to a clearer explanation.

Learners submit a question, choose a home language, and receive a translation with an option to ask an AI tutor for help.

FOR LEARNERS

Instant question support

Capture or upload a question, select a language, and get accessible wording plus step-by-step explanations.

FOR TEACHERS

Reviewed class resources

Translate a full paper, check it side by side, then publish the approved version to a class.

The AI translates explanatory wording only. Formulas, values, units, question numbers, and marks remain unchanged.

03 · Learner journey

Five steps from upload to understanding.

Submit

Photograph or upload a Maths or Science question.

Choose

Select the language the learner wants to use.

Read

AI checks clarity and extracts the question content.

Translate

Question wording is translated while notation is preserved.

Learn

Read the result and ask follow-up questions.

If the image is blurry or incomplete, the learner is asked to retake or replace it.

06 · Teacher paper workflow

Human review before a paper is shared.

Full-paper translation is a separate batch process. Teacher approval is the gate before publishing.

STEP 01

Upload

Teacher submits a full paper for processing.

STEP 02

Translate

AI extracts and translates the document.

STEP 03

Review & approve

Teacher checks the side-by-side content and approves it.

STEP 04

Publish & notify

Approved material is shared; n8n can send class alerts.

07 · Quality and safety

Helpful AI needs careful checks.

PRESERVE

Keep technical notation intact

Protect formulas, numbers, units, symbols, question numbering, marks, and diagram labels.

BE HONEST

Do not guess missing content

Ask for a clearer image or route uncertain output for review instead of inventing text.

PROTECT

Secure learner information

Authenticate workflows, manage credentials securely, and limit access to uploads and chat history.

REVIEW

Keep teachers in the loop

Review and approve full-paper translations before publication or notifications.
