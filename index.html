Context & Potential

The Language Barrier in STEM Education

Over 80% of South African learners speak an indigenous language at home. When assessments are strictly administered in formal English, mathematical potential is lost to linguistic complexity.

Problem Statement

High school students find it hard to learn and understand Mathematics and Physical Sciences, especially those from disadvantaged and rural backgrounds, due to language barriers and the language of instruction.

  • × Linguistic Friction: Learners fail assessments not because they cannot solve the problem, but because they do not understand the English question phrasing.
  • × Educator Capacity Constraints: Teachers do not have the time to manually translate question papers across 5 to 10 indigenous home languages.
  • × Generic Translation Failures: Standard tools alter chemical notation, break exponents, and corrupt question numbering ($x^2 \rightarrow x2$, $\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{H2O}$).
  • × Parental Support Gap: Parents who do not understand technical English are unable to assist their children with schoolwork.

The Opportunity

Providing learners with a direct system that simplifies their educational journey by enabling them to learn in the language they naturally think in.

  • • Empowering Disadvantaged Learners: Unlocking STEM career pathways for youth in township and rural school districts.
  • • Real-Time Self-Reliance: Students capture questions independently during homework or exam prep without waiting for manual teacher translation.
  • • Mathematical Precision: Preserving scientific notation, SI units, and structural question marks completely intact.
  • • Curriculum Alignment: Formatted to fully respect the South African Department of Basic Education (DBE) CAPS standards.
System Design

The Proposed Solution: iFundza AI

An AI-powered multimodal platform enabling students to upload or capture a question, analyze the input, and receive a translated question with step-by-step guidance.

Flow A: Learner Real-Time Vision (Primary)

The learner takes a photo of a question from a textbook, whiteboard, or worksheet. The multimodal AI extracts text, diagrams, and equations, translates the question to their home language, and provides an immediate explanation option.

  • • Image & Photo Capture: Direct ingestion of textbook, whiteboard, or past paper photos.
  • • WhatsApp Ingestion: Optional submission via WhatsApp for low-data learners.
  • • Multimodal OCR: Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision extracts formulas and diagrams simultaneously.

Flow B: Teacher Class Papers (Batch)

Educators upload full examination papers in PDF or Word format. The system automatically translates the document, offers an approval editor, and publishes downloadable PDFs to learners.

  • • Full Examination Processing: Automated multi-page question paper translation in under 2 minutes.
  • • Teacher Audit & Approval: Side-by-side verification before class-wide publication.
  • • Broadcast Notifications: Automated notifications delivered to learners and parents.
Workflow

How iFundza AI Works

A streamlined, four-step multimodal translation pipeline.

STEP 01

Capture or Upload

The learner captures a photo of a question from a textbook, exam paper, or classroom board.

STEP 02

Vision Analysis

The vision model analyzes English instructions, algebraic expressions, and scientific figures.

STEP 03

Formula Protection

Translates question text into the chosen language. Numbers, formulas, and marks stay untouched.

STEP 04

AI Explanation

Learners can request step-by-step conceptual explanations in their own home language.
