Flow A: Learner Real-Time Vision (Primary)

  • Capture / Upload: Photograph question from textbook, whiteboard, or exam paper.
  • Multimodal Vision: Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts text, math equations, and diagram labels simultaneously.
  • Instant Translation: Translates to isiZulu, Sepedi, Setswana, etc. while preserving all formulas and marks.
  • AI Chat Tutor: Learners can request step-by-step conceptual coaching in their home language.

Flow B: Teacher Class Papers (Batch)

  • Full Papers: Teachers upload complete question papers in PDF or Word format.
  • Review and Audit: Teachers verify translations side-by-side prior to publishing.
  • Notifications: Broadcasts announcements to students and parents via WhatsApp and Email.

Multimodal Translation Rules

  • Clarity Verification: Detects blurry or cropped photos and prompts learner to retake.
  • Formula Protection: Algebraic variables, powers, and chemical equations are strictly preserved.
  • Diagram Integrity: Diagram labels stay in original format while explanatory notes translate.
